Subtítulo: The Power of 3 Unveils Their Debut

A new supergroup, The Power of 3, has announced their self-titled debut album, set to release on December 11th via She2Music. Featuring Orianthi, Cindy Blackman Santana, and Rhonda Smith, this electrifying trio promises a powerful blend of bluesy rock and virtuosic performances.

Ahead of its release, the band has unveiled two songs, lead single ‘War in the Streets’ backed with ‘Treat Me Like a Lady.’ The three virtuosos dig into the track without mind-blowing pyrotechnics, focusing on the unique magic of three greats jamming on a great riff.

The bass especially is active and deep in the pocket, with the drums producing an energetic core. The closing minute is a lengthy guitar solo from Orianthi that’s as singable and hooky as any of the vocal melodies.

02 · Subtítulo: The Power of 3’s Superstar Musicians

Subtítulo: The Power of 3’s Superstar Musicians

The three musicians behind The Power of 3 are a powerhouse in their own right. Orianthi, the original selected guitarist for Michael Jackson's concert residency and played with Alice Cooper and carved out an impressive solo career, has joined forces with Cindy Blackman Santana and Rhonda Smith.

Cindy Blackman Santana has played with greats ranging from her husband Carlos Santana to Pharoah Sanders to John McLaughlin, and landed at No. 10 in the charts. Rhonda Smith played extensively with Prince and Jeff Beck, and came in at No. 24 on the charts.

Orianthi stated of the project, ‘I know people all over the world want to see us.’ Playing together with Cindy and Rhonda, this is like a powerhouse.
