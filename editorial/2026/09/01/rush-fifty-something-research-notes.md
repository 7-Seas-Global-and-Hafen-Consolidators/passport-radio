# Rush · Fifty Something · editorial research notes

Internal research notes. Not rendered as article credits.

- 2026-08-31: Rush official site announced four additional 2026 dates: Houston (Oct 1), St. Louis (Oct 21), Cincinnati (Oct 23), Pittsburgh (Nov 15).
- Official explanation: original routing was deliberately cautious because Geddy Lee and Alex Lifeson did not know how they would hold up to touring; they reported doing well and having fun.
- 2026 tour uses two-set evening-with format and a rotating pool of 40+ songs.
- First show of Fifty Something: Kia Forum, Los Angeles/Inglewood, 2026-06-07, first Rush concert in nearly 11 years.
- Performance lineup: Geddy Lee (vocals/bass/keys), Alex Lifeson (guitar/vocals), Anika Nilles (drums), Loren Gold (keyboards).
- Anika Nilles is a German drummer/composer/producer/studio musician and toured with Jeff Beck in 2022.
- First-show set included Xanadu, Limelight, Far Cry, Subdivisions, Freewill, Bravado, Caravan, La Villa Strangiato, Vital Signs, The Spirit of Radio, 2112 sections, Distant Early Warning, Red Barchetta, Dreamline, Natural Science, Time Stand Still with Aimee Mann, Red Sector A, YYZ, The Garden, Tom Sawyer, By-Tor & The Snow Dog, Working Man.
